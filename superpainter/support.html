Contact us

Send us an email at bytes.inno@gmail.com and tell us which device and iOS version you use, plus what happened. If your question is about a purchase, please include the product name (Pro Weekly / Pro Monthly / Pro Yearly / Lifetime).

Common topics

  • Subscriptions & billing — subscriptions are managed by Apple. To cancel or change a plan, open your device's Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. Cancelling stops future renewals; Pro stays active until the end of the period you already paid for.
  • Restore a purchase — open the App, go to the Pro membership page and tap Restore purchases. This works for subscriptions and for the one-time Lifetime purchase.
  • Refunds — requests are handled by Apple, not by us. Please use reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Change the app language — open Me → Settings → Language and pick one of the 8 supported languages (English, 简体中文, 繁體中文, 日本語, 한국어, Deutsch, Français, Español).
  • Your photos — generating a drawing from a photo happens entirely on your device. Photos are never uploaded to us.
  • Your progress — colouring progress is saved on your device, and synchronised to your account when you are signed in, so it survives a reinstall.
  • Delete your account — Me → Edit profile → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, your community posts and your local data.
  • Report or block someone — long-press a post in the community feed (Moments) and choose Report or Block. We review reports within 24 hours.

Reporting objectionable content

Super Painter's community feed is user-generated. We do not tolerate objectionable content: use the in-app report action (or email us) and we will remove violating content and, where appropriate, suspend or permanently ban the account responsible — within 24 hours of the report. You can also block any user so their content is hidden from you.
