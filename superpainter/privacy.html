Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Photo Paint is a paint-by-numbers colouring app. You can use the core experience without an account. The photos you use to create a drawing are processed entirely on your device and are never uploaded. We do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and we do not track you across apps.

1. Who we are and what this policy covers

This Privacy Policy applies to the Photo Paint mobile app (the "App"), published by the developer of Photo Paint ("we", "us"). It explains what information the App collects, why, where it is stored, and the choices you have. It applies to the App itself, not to services operated by Apple or by other companies whose terms you separately accept.

2. Information we collect

a) Account and profile. On first launch the App signs you in anonymously through Google Firebase Authentication, which creates a random account identifier. That identifier contains no name, phone number or email address. You may optionally link a Google or Apple account to keep your data across devices. If you do, the provider shares an identifier for your account and basic profile information (such as your name and, depending on what you allow, your email address); we use it only to sign you in and to show which account is linked. A nickname and an optional short bio are stored with your profile (the nickname is also printed as a watermark on the artwork you export).

b) Content you post. The App has a community feed ("Moments"). If you publish a drawing, text, thumbnail or drawing-process video there, we store that content, together with the associated comments and likes, so it can be shown to other users. Publishing is always your choice — anything you never publish stays on your device. You can delete your posts and comments, and you can block or report other users.

c) Drawing progress and in-app activity. We store your colouring progress per drawing, time spent, completed works, points, items, achievements and app settings. Progress is kept in a local database on your device; when you are signed in, it is also synchronised to our cloud backend so that reinstalling the App or changing devices does not lose your work.

d) Purchases. Membership subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed by Apple. We never receive your card details or your Apple ID password. We receive the purchase result (which product was bought, and its status) from the App Store so we can unlock the corresponding features, and we keep a record of your membership on your device.

e) Diagnostics. The App uses Google Firebase Crashlytics to report crashes and errors (for example: crash stack traces, app version and device model, and short technical log lines). Crash reports contain no photos and no artwork content. We do not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs, and we do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA).

3. Photos, camera and your photo library

  • Creating a drawing from a photo. You pick a photo from your photo library. Decoding, analysis and drawing generation all happen on your device. The photo is not uploaded to us or to anyone else, and it is not stored in our cloud backend.
  • Drawing from scratch. The App does not use your camera; there is no camera capture feature.
  • Saving your finished artwork. When you choose to save or export a finished piece (or its creation video), the App writes the file to your photo library at your request, and/or hands it to the system share sheet so you can send it where you like. We do not receive a copy.

4. What we do not collect

  • Your photos or videos as uploads — photo processing happens only on your device;
  • Precise or approximate location, contacts, calendars, SMS or call logs;
  • Microphone, health, or fitness data;
  • Advertising identifiers, and any data used to track you across other apps or websites;
  • Payment card numbers or bank details (Apple handles payments).

5. Where your data is stored

  • On your device: drawing progress, settings, points and items, achievements, and your membership record, in a local database.
  • In the cloud (if you are signed in / publish): your account identifier, nickname, bio, profile photo (if any), your community posts, comments and likes, and your drawing progress, stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Authentication). Crash diagnostics are processed by Firebase Crashlytics. These services are operated by Google LLC and their servers are primarily located in the United States.

We keep cloud data for as long as you use the App. When you delete content or your account, it is removed from our cloud backend (see section 7).

6. How we use information, and who we share it with

We use the information described above only to operate the App: to sign you in, to keep your progress, to run the community feed, to deliver the features you paid for, to keep the service secure and to fix crashes. We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for advertising.

The data is processed by the following service providers on our behalf:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Crashlytics) — hosting, database, sign-in and crash reporting;
  • Apple — App Store payments, subscriptions and receipts;
  • Google / Apple sign-in — only if you choose to link an account.

We may also disclose information if we are legally required to do so, or where necessary to protect the rights and safety of users.

7. Your choices and your rights

  • Use the App without an account: the core colouring experience works offline and without signing in; the community feed is the part that needs a network connection.
  • Edit or clear your profile: change or empty your nickname and bio in the App's settings.
  • Delete content: delete your own posts and comments in the community feed; report or block other users from the same menu.
  • Delete your account: in the App, go to Me → Edit profile → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, your cloud content (profile, posts, comments, likes) and your local data, including spent points and items. This cannot be undone.
  • Access, correction and portability: you can export your finished artwork from within the App. For any other request (including a copy or correction of your data, or an objection to processing), contact us at the address below; we respond within 24 hours and will act within the time limits required by applicable law (for example the GDPR, where we rely on contract performance and legitimate interests as our legal bases).

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable age of consent in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS), and access to our cloud database is restricted by security rules so that you can only read and modify your own records. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and to keep the App's handling of it minimal.

10. International transfers

Our service providers may process your information in countries other than your own (primarily the United States). Where required, transfers rely on the safeguards offered by those providers, such as standard contractual clauses.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example if we add a feature that changes what we collect. Changes take effect when posted at this URL, and the date at the top of this page is updated.

12. Contact

Questions, requests or complaints about privacy: bytes.inno@gmail.com. We respond within 24 hours.
