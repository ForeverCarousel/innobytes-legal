The Art Stall is the marketplace inside Photo Paint where you can offer a drawing you generated from your own photo, and where other users can buy drawings with in-app points. These rules form part of our Terms of Use; what happens to your photo when you post is described in the Privacy Policy. Points are virtual goods: they have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for money.

1. Posting a drawing

  • What you may post. Drawings you generated in the App from a photo you own or have the right to use. You must not post anything unlawful or infringing, sexually explicit, violent or graphic, hateful, harassing, defamatory or bullying, spam or advertising, or anything that violates another person's privacy or publicity rights.
  • Your promises. By posting you confirm that you own the rights to the photo, that it does not show another person's face (or that you have their consent) and that it contains no copyrighted material belonging to anyone else.
  • Your photo becomes public. Posting uploads the drawing assets (region data, line art, preview image and metadata) and the photo you created the drawing from to our cloud storage, where buyers can download them. Before uploading, the App shows you the actual preview buyers will see and asks you to confirm. Uploaded photos are re-encoded with EXIF location data stripped and are subject to size limits (source photo ≤ 3 MB, snapshot ≤ 8 MB per drawing).
  • Review the rules first. The first time you post you must read these rules and tick "I have read and agree".

3. Commission and settlement

  • We take a 20% commission on every sale. Those points are burned — they are not kept by us. For example, a drawing sold for 30 points earns you 24 points.
  • Your earnings stay pending for 24 hours after the sale and are then settled into your points balance. The App shows the pending amount and the time it becomes available. Settlement can be withheld or reversed if an order is refunded or frozen for review.
  • There is no daily cap on what you can earn; the limits in section 2 and the anti-abuse rules in section 5 are the brakes that keep the economy healthy.

5. No self-purchase, no trading rings

Points must not be farmed. The following are prohibited and are checked automatically:

  • You may not buy your own drawing.
  • The same buyer counts for at most 3 orders to the same seller within 7 days.
  • If two users buy from each other within 24 hours, both accounts' earnings are frozen for review.
  • Abnormal activity (for example more than 5 orders in a minute) freezes the earnings involved until they are reviewed.

Frozen earnings are not settled while they are under review, and points obtained by abuse may be reversed. Repeated or serious abuse leads to unlisting and to a permanent ban.

6. No resale

A drawing you buy is licensed to you for your own colouring and for saving the finished artwork. It stays colourable forever, but you may not post it again, resell it or transfer it to anyone else. The App disables the "post to the Art Stall" action for drawings you bought.

7. Violating content: reporting, take-down and legal consequences

The community feed and the Art Stall are user-generated content, and the App provides the moderation tools Apple requires for them:

  • Reporting. Every listing has a report action (with a reason and an optional description). You can also email us at the address below.
  • Response time. We review reports and act within 24 hours.
  • Actions we can take. We may unlist or remove a drawing, restrict an account, and permanently ban accounts that repeatedly or seriously break these rules. Where an upload infringes someone else's rights we reserve the right to pursue legal remedies.
  • Blocking. You can block another user so their content is hidden from you.
  • Appeals. If you disagree with a moderation decision or a refund, you can appeal in the App (or by email); we reply within 24 hours.

8. Changes to these rules

We may update these rules as the Art Stall evolves (for example to adjust limits or to keep the economy healthy). The updated rules take effect when posted at this URL, and the date at the top of this page is updated. If you do not agree with a change, stop posting drawings to the Art Stall.
