Delete your Photo Paint account

Last updated: October 17, 2026
HomePrivacy PolicyTerms of UseSupport

You can delete your Photo Paint account and its data at any time. There are two ways:

Option 1 — In the app (fastest)

Open Photo Paint → Settings (Me) → Account → Delete account, then confirm. Your account is deleted immediately and your data is removed as described below.

Option 2 — By email

Email bytes.inno@gmail.com from the address associated with your account (or include your in-app user ID found in Settings → About). We will process the request within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account record (display name, avatar choice, member status)
  • Your in-progress and completed drawings stored on your device and in the cloud
  • Your points balance and achievements progress
  • Your community posts (Moments) and their embedded images
  • Every art-stall listing you created, and its snapshot files in cloud storage

What is kept, and why

  • Purchase records (what you bought and when) are retained as required for tax and accounting purposes. They are not used for any other purpose.
  • Copies a buyer already purchased from you stay in that buyer's library — deleting your account does not revoke what someone else legitimately bought. Those copies no longer show your name or avatar.
  • Aggregated, non-identifying statistics (e.g. total drawings completed) may be retained.

Deleting only some data

You do not have to delete your whole account. You can, without deleting anything else:

  • Remove a single drawing (library → drawing → delete)
  • Delete an individual post in Moments
  • Unlist a listing from the art stall (it is removed from the store immediately)

Contact
